OUR MISSION

Making neighbourhoods healthier, one report at a time

Litter, blocked drains, illegal dumping and other cleanliness problems affect everyone’s quality of life. CleanAlert gives residents a clear voice and gives local teams the visibility they need to act quickly.

01

For Residents

Spot an issue? Open the app, choose a category, add a short description, location and optional photo. Submit in under a minute and track the status of every report you make.

02

For Administrators

Review incoming reports, update their status (pending, in progress, resolved or declined), and coordinate clean-up efforts with a clear overview of what needs attention most.

03

For the Community

Every resolved report makes shared spaces safer and healthier. Transparent tracking builds trust and encourages more people to get involved.
