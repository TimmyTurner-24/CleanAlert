About CleanAlert

Our Mission

CleanAlert is a community-driven platform dedicated to keeping our neighborhoods clean and healthy. We empower residents to report environmental issues and enable administrators to track and resolve them efficiently.

How It Works

For Residents

Report cleanliness issues in your area with images and descriptions. Track the status of your reports and see how the community is improving.

For Administrators

Review and manage reports from residents. Update status and coordinate cleanup efforts to maintain community standards.

Key Features

  • 🔍 Easy report submission with photo uploads
  • 📊 Real-time status tracking
  • 🔐 Secure account management
  • 📈 Community impact statistics
  • ✅ Categorized issue tracking

Get Involved

Join us in making our community cleaner and healthier. Create an account, report issues, and be part of the solution!
